Axon Voice

A local-first voice work partner that answers from evidence and learns from you.

voice → Whisper → parallel retrieval (vector memory · knowledge base · Neo4j graph) with RRF fusion → local LLM (Ollama) or cloud → speech, sentence by sentence

It cites the exact page or says it does not know (corrective RAG with LangGraph). What you state goes into a time-aware knowledge graph. An always-on-top orb: hold Right Ctrl to talk from any app.
